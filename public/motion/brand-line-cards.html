Move your pointer across the card to scatter its lines. Detection boxes change their positions, sizes and connections as you move. On a touch screen, tap the artwork. Keyboard focus also reveals the detection boxes.

One SVG path, sampled once into a brightness grid. The large logo is a field of vertical lines; the small header mark uses the original vector path. Four stroke widths, a drifting noise field, and a spring at each joint bring the signal to life.
