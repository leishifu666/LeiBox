# ASCII Shark Price Paths / 字符鲨鱼追踪价格路径

An ASCII hammerhead swims along three forecast price paths and tags each with its odds, volatility and dealer gamma; the pointer steers it to the nearest path, or lets it swim freely over the candle history.

字符锤头鲨沿三条预测价格路径游动，依次标记概率、波动率与 dealer gamma；指针在预测区时鲨鱼追向最近的路径，移到左侧K线区时鲨鱼跟着指针自由游动。
