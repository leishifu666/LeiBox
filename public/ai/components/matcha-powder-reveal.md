# Package Tear and Powder Reveal / 包装撕开粒子消散

Drag the upper-right notch to peel open a solid 3D foil pouch. Stop halfway, regrab the bent strip and finish tearing to release the powder.

从右上角拖开立体金属包装，可停在半途并继续拉动弯折封条，完全撕开后释放抹茶粉末。
