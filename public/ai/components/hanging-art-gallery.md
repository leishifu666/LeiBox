# 3D Hanging Art Gallery / 3D 悬挂画廊

Five framed artworks hang from a chrome rail. Hover to turn one forward as the others reveal wood, lacquer, gold, terracotta and celadon backs.

五幅画沿金属横杆悬挂，悬停时翻到正面，其余画框露出木纹、黑漆、古金、红陶和青瓷背面。
