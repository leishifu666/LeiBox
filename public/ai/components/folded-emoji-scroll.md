# Folded Emoji Scroll / 折边滚动表情选择器

A searchable emoji grid folds its actual content into the top and bottom edges as you scroll, while the search field stays fixed.

表情网格滚入上下边缘时向内弯折、压缩，搜索栏保持固定，支持搜索与选择。
