# Staggered Radial Menu / 环形菜单交错展开

Six coloured actions arrive from the outside in clockwise order, spring into a ring, then scatter outwards in reverse with soft blur.

六个彩色按钮从外围顺时针依次收拢成环，弹性停稳；关闭时逆序向外散开并模糊淡出。
