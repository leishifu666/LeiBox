# Ink Spread Carousel / 墨水扩散图片轮播

A new photo spreads from your press with a rippling ink edge and a thin lens distortion, while a soft image shadow changes behind the white card.

新图从点击位置沿不规则墨水边缘向外晕开，边界带有轻微透镜折射，白色卡片后的柔焦色影同步换色。
