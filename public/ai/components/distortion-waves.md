# Distortion Waves / 折射波浪视图转场

A colourful WebGL2 wave pulls toward the trigger, bends the live DOM with chromatic aberration and blur, and carries one view into another.

彩色 WebGL2 波浪先向触发按钮蓄力内吸，随后带着实时 DOM 折射、色散、模糊与边缘光晕扫过卡片并切换视图。
