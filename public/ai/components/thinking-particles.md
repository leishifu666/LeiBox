# Thinking Step Particle Morph / 思考步骤粒子变形

Particles morph through five thinking steps, then collapse into a streamed answer with replay and copy controls.

粒子随五个思考步骤连续变形，完成后收起并逐字显示回答，支持暂停、重播和复制。
