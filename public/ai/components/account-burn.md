# Hold-to-Burn Transition / 长按燃烧转场

Hold to ignite a paper-burn transition with fluid fire and drifting ash. Restore to pull the burn back into the button.

长按点燃纸张，流体火焰和灰烬向外烧开画面。恢复时，燃烧边缘反向收回按钮。
